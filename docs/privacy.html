Glyph Pet — Privacy policy

Developer: n7 · Last updated: November 2, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Glyph Pet does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your pet, its needs, your eggs and your collection are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Sensors

The app reads the accelerometer while the Glyph Toy is active, only to react to tilting and shaking. This data is used on the device and never leaves it.

Purchases

The optional "Supporter" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether the purchase was made.

Children

Glyph Pet is intended for users aged 13 and over.
