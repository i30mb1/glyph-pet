New egg game: every day you play, a new pet arrives as an egg, never one you already have. Press it five times and it hatches. Supporters get every missing pet as an egg at once. Pets now show how they feel: a hungry one thinks of food, a sad one cries, a happy one hops, and a pet left alone too long gets sick until you feed it. Each of the 18 has its own trick, and in the app you can swipe through everything your pet can do.
