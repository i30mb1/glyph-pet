Ten new pets: snail, hedgehog, crab, whale, bee, bat, chameleon, axolotl, gem and planet, 28 in all. New controls: hold the Glyph button to feed, rock the phone gently to play, shake it to grow a ready pet. The matrix goes dark face up or in a pocket and lights up when you pick the phone up. The pet looks up at a notification (it sees only its kind, never the text) and shows a phone for a missed call. Dropped the phone? It says ouch and wears a plaster until tomorrow.
