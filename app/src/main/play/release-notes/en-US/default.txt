The bunny has come alive: its ears hang on a spring when you lean the phone, trail in a hop and stand up at a clap, and left alone it settles into a loaf. Its new game, the carrot patch: lean to hop to the carrot top, nod to pull it out. The black hole is redrawn: a big core with eyes, a solid disc that rocks after the phone, a star for dinner drawn out into a stream. Tentacles, stems and tails are lit tubes with a travelling wave.
