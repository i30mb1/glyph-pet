Glyph Pet turns the Glyph Matrix on the back of your Nothing Phone (3) into a home for a tiny pet.

A NEW PET EVERY DAY
Every day you play, a new pet arrives as an egg, always one you do not have yet. Put it on the matrix in the app and hold the Glyph button five times: it hatches. Eggs you do not open wait for you. Your very first pet starts as an egg too.

NO BUTTONS ON THE SCREEN
Hold the Glyph button and your pet eats. Rock the phone gently from side to side and you play together. Shake it and a pet that is ready grows up. The app shows how to fill each need right next to it.

IT HAS FEELINGS
Your pet gets hungry, sleepy and bored. It sleeps at night, works out while the phone charges, gets dizzy when you shake the phone and rolls when you tilt it. It never dies.

COLLECT ALL 18
10 common, 5 rare and 3 legendary pets. Common ones come most often, legendary ones rarely, and never a pet you already have. Pick any pet you have collected to live on the matrix.

ALWAYS WITH YOU
Set Glyph Pet as your Always-on Glyph Toy and it stays on the back of your phone.

SUPPORTER
An optional one-time purchase: every pet you do not have yet arrives as an egg right away. Everything else is free.

Made for the Nothing Phone (3) Glyph Matrix.
