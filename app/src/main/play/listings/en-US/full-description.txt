Glyph Pet turns the Glyph Matrix on the back of your Nothing Phone (3) into a home for a tiny pet.

AN EGG EVERY DAY
Every morning at 6:00 a new egg appears. By the evening it hatches. Who is inside is a surprise: one of 18 pets.

ONE BUTTON
Hold the Glyph button and your pet eats, cheers up, or the egg hatches sooner. The app always tells you what the button will do right now.

IT HAS FEELINGS
Your pet gets hungry, sleepy and bored. It sleeps at night and on the charger, gets dizzy when you shake the phone and rolls when you tilt it. It never dies.

COLLECT ALL 18
10 common, 5 rare and 3 legendary pets. The odds are shown in the app. Duplicates count too.

ALWAYS WITH YOU
Set Glyph Pet as your Always-on Glyph Toy and it stays on the back of your phone.

SUPPORTER
An optional one-time purchase: a second egg every day, golden eggs and one instant hatch a day. Everything else is free.

Made for the Nothing Phone (3) Glyph Matrix.
