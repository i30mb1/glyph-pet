Glyph Pet is a little pet that lives on your phone: 72 pets, each with its own animations, games and toys, on any Android phone. On a Nothing Phone (3) it also moves onto the Glyph Matrix on the back.

A NEW PET EVERY DAY
Every day you play, a new pet comes as an egg, always one you do not have yet. Hold it five times, the circle in the app or the Glyph button on the matrix, or just shake the phone: it hatches. Eggs you have not opened wait for you.

EVERY PET ITS OWN WAY
72 pets and more than 500 animations. Each one eats, sleeps, cheers up, sulks, charges, takes a cuddle and rolls down a lean in its own way, and each has a signature effect. The cat purrs, the cactus blooms, the whale spouts, and the owl's head really does go round.

A GROWN-UP LIVES ON ITS OWN
A pet you have grown up needs nothing: it is always fed and content, and nobody calls you to feed it. It just keeps you company and now and then gets on with its own things, two pastimes of its own for every pet. The lion shakes its mane, the hare munches a carrot, the firefly knocks on the lid of its jar, the cloud snows instead of raining. And while you are together, it brings new eggs.

REAL MINI-GAMES
Every pet has its own game on the matrix, steered by tilting the phone: roll a ball through a maze, beam up a cow with the UFO, find a moth by its echo.

A TOY WHILE YOU TYPE
Lay the phone matrix up by your keyboard and type: every pet builds something of its own to your keys, a garden, an igloo, a coral reef, a burrow. The faster you type, the faster it grows, and at about 10 and 20 seconds it builds to a finale. Pause, and the scene waits for you.

SIMPLE TO PLAY
Hold the circle in the app, or the Glyph button on a Nothing Phone (3): your pet eats, and a full one starts its game. Tap the back twice: a cuddle. Shake: its signature effect. Blow, clap or type nearby: it answers.

GROWS WHILE YOU ARE TOGETHER
Drop by for a couple of minutes when you have time: a baby becomes a teen in 15 minutes and an adult an hour later. While it grows, feed it and play with it; one action is one dot on a meter, and the app shows how to fill each one. The album shows which animations you have seen and which are still a question mark. Your pet never dies.

COLLECT ALL 72
26 common, 29 rare, 4 legendary and 5 mythic, and 8 anomalies you find by doing their thing: charge to 100% for the phoenix, wait for the full moon for the moon rabbit. Legendary and mythic pets live on the matrix in 3D. Any pet you have collected can move onto the matrix.

ALWAYS WITH YOU
Make Glyph Pet your Always-on Glyph Toy and it stays on the back of your phone, fully alive.

SUPPORTER
An optional one-time purchase: every pet you do not have yet arrives as an egg at once, the anomalies too, and every new pet an update brings. Everything else is free.

For any Android phone, and at its best on the Glyph Matrix of the Nothing Phone (3).
