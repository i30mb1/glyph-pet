Glyph Pet turns the Glyph Matrix on the back of the Nothing Phone (3) into a home for a little pet. On a phone without the matrix, you can play right in the app.

A NEW PET EVERY DAY
Every day you play, a new pet comes as an egg, always one you do not have yet. Put it on the matrix and hold the Glyph button five times: it hatches. Eggs you have not opened wait for you.

EVERY PET ITS OWN WAY
29 pets and more than 400 animations. Each one eats, sleeps, cheers up, sulks, charges, takes a cuddle and rolls down a lean in its own way, and each has a signature effect. The cat purrs, the cactus blooms, the whale spouts, and the owl's head really does go round.

REAL MINI-GAMES
Every pet has its own game on the matrix, steered by tilting the phone: roll a ball through a maze, beam up a cow with the UFO, find a moth by its echo.

SIMPLE TO PLAY
Hold the Glyph button: your pet eats, and a full one starts its game. Tap the back twice: a cuddle. Shake: its signature effect. One action is one dot on a meter, and the app shows how to fill each one.

GROWS WHILE YOU ARE TOGETHER
Drop by for a couple of minutes when you have time: a baby becomes a teen in 15 minutes and an adult an hour later. The album shows which animations you have seen and which are still a question mark. Your pet never dies.

COLLECT ALL 29
10 common, 13 rare, 4 legendary and 2 mythic. Legendary and mythic pets live on the matrix in 3D. Any pet you have collected can move onto the matrix.

ALWAYS WITH YOU
Make Glyph Pet your Always-on Glyph Toy and it stays on the back of your phone.

SUPPORTER
An optional one-time purchase: every pet you do not have yet arrives as an egg at once. Everything else is free.

Made for the Glyph Matrix of the Nothing Phone (3).
