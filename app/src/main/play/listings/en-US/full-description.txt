Glyph Pet turns the Glyph Matrix on the back of your Nothing Phone (3) into a home for a tiny pet.

A NEW PET EVERY DAY
Every day you play, a new pet arrives as an egg, always one you do not have yet. Press the egg five times and it hatches: tap it in the app or hold the Glyph button. Eggs you do not open wait for you. Your very first pet starts as an egg too.

ONE BUTTON
Hold the Glyph button and the egg cracks, or your pet eats or cheers up. The app always tells you what the button will do right now.

IT HAS FEELINGS
Your pet gets hungry, sleepy and bored. It sleeps at night, works out while the phone charges, gets dizzy when you shake the phone and rolls when you tilt it. It never dies.

COLLECT ALL 18
10 common, 5 rare and 3 legendary pets. Common ones come most often, legendary ones rarely, and never a pet you already have. Pick any pet you have collected to live on the matrix.

ALWAYS WITH YOU
Set Glyph Pet as your Always-on Glyph Toy and it stays on the back of your phone.

SUPPORTER
An optional one-time purchase: every pet you do not have yet arrives as an egg right away, and your eggs shine brighter on the matrix. Everything else is free.

Made for the Nothing Phone (3) Glyph Matrix.
